Official Corridor Stops & Intermediate Stations

Boarding and drop-off stations approved under LTFRB Express Franchise: Sta. Cruz ↔ Sapang Palay via Muzon, Marilao Exit.

8 Authorized Stations
01

Sta. Cruz Terminal (Avenida, Manila)

Manila Origin Hub

Rizal Avenue corner Doroteo Jose St., near LRT-1 Doroteo Jose and LRT-2 Recto stations. Primary southern terminal dispatch with ticket counter and passenger lounge.

KM 0.00 LRT-1 & LRT-2 Transfer Main Manila Dispatch Bay
02

Monumento / 5th Avenue (Caloocan)

Metro Manila Hub

Bonifacio Monument Circle, Rizal Avenue Extension, EDSA. Convenient transfer for Caloocan, Malabon, and Navotas commuters.

KM 6.50 Bonifacio Circle EDSA / CAMANAVA
03

Balintawak Transit Hub (NLEX Entry)

Interchange Hub

Cloverleaf interchange, Ayala Malls Cloverleaf, LRT-1 Balintawak station walkway. Direct gateway entering the North Luzon Expressway (NLEX).

KM 9.80 Ayala Malls Access NLEX Express Entry
04

Marilao Exit (NLEX Toll Plaza)

Express NLEX Exit

NLEX Marilao interchange, Patubig, Vitarich junction. Express buses exit NLEX directly onto Marilao-San Jose del Monte Provincial Road, bypassing heavy local city traffic.

KM 23.50 NLEX Toll Plaza Patubig Junction
05

Muzon Commercial Junction

Major Bulacan Hub

St. Joseph the Worker Parish, Muzon Public Market, crossing to Marilao and Santa Maria. Highest-volume boarding and turnstile node in San Jose del Monte.

KM 32.00 Muzon Public Market High Commuter Exchange
06

Tungko / SM San Jose del Monte

Commercial Gateway

Tungkong Mangga terminal interchange, SM City SJDM, Quirino Highway intersection. Core commercial district of SJDM.

KM 37.50 SM SJDM Terminal Quirino Hwy Crossing
07

San Jose del Monte City Hall

Civic Center

Bulacan Government Center, Dulong Bayan, City College of San Jose del Monte (CCSJDM). Convenient for civil servants, students, and local residents.

KM 41.00 Government Center City College (CCSJDM)
08

Sapang Palay Central Terminal

Destination Hub

FVR Central Terminal (Area E / Sapang Palay Proper). Main Bonhagan Transit dispatch depot with overnight bus bays, passenger lounge, maintenance shop, and dispatch management.

KM 45.20 Central Depot Customer Dispatch Bay
