Corporate Profile & Operations

Connecting Bulacan to the Metro with Dignity and Punctuality

Founded to bring structured scheduling, online seat assurances, and reliable express connectivity to the Monumento ↔ San Jose del Monte transit corridor.

Our Mission

To modernize public road transport along Quirino Highway by eliminating passenger uncertainty. Every commuter deserves a guaranteed seat, transparent fares, and on-time departures.

Our Vision

To be Northern Metro Manila and Bulacan's most respected passenger bus operator, leading digital ticketing integration, driver wellness, and low-emission fleet modernization.

Safety & Integrity

100% compliant with LTFRB and DOTr public utility standards. Every coach undergoes daily mechanical inspection, speed limiting, and complete interior sanitation before rollout.
