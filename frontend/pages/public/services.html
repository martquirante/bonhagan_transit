20% Statutory Passenger Discount Policy

In full compliance with Philippine transportation laws

Students (RA 11314)

Applies to currently enrolled basic education, high school, vocational, and undergraduate college students. Present a valid validated school ID during boarding or upload to your passenger profile.

Senior Citizens (RA 9994)

Applies to Filipino citizens aged 60 years and above. Honored on all trips upon presentation of official OSCA ID, Philippine Passport, or government-issued photo ID showing birthdate.

Persons with Disabilities (RA 10754)

Applies to passengers with registered disabilities. Validated through official PWD identification cards issued by the National Council on Disability Affairs (NCDA) or LGU Mayor's Office.
